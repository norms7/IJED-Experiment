WHO WE ARE

About Infant Jesus Learning Academy

Infant Jesus Learning Academy (IJED) is a Catholic educational institution committed to forming well-rounded individuals rooted in faith, excellence, and service. Founded with the mission of providing quality education, IJED nurtures students in a caring and disciplined environment.

⛪
Our Mission
To provide holistic, values-based education that empowers every learner to achieve academic excellence and become a responsible member of society.
👁️
Our Vision
To be a leading Catholic learning institution that produces globally competitive, morally upright, and socially responsible graduates.
✝️
Our Values
Faith, integrity, excellence, compassion, and service are the core values that guide every student, teacher, and staff member of IJED.
